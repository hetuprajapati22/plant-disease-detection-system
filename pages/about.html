About TomatoGuard AI

A symptom-based tomato plant disease detection system built to help farmers and gardeners spot plant problems early.

Project Overview

TomatoGuard AI lets a user select the symptoms they notice on a tomato plant, such as spots on leaves, yellowing, curling or webbing. The system compares these symptoms with a tomato disease symptom dataset and shows the most likely disease or pest, along with a match score and basic care suggestions.

Results are saved in a detection history so users can review their earlier checks, and a detailed report can be printed for reference.

Project Objective

  • Help users identify possible tomato plant problems early
  • Provide simple information about symptoms, causes and prevention
  • Give basic care suggestions in an easy-to-understand way
  • Show how AI can support farmers and gardeners
❓

Problem Statement

Many tomato diseases look similar in their early stage, and small farmers often do not have quick access to an expert. Late identification can spread the problem to the whole crop and reduce the harvest. TomatoGuard AI aims to give a quick, easy-to-use first guidance based on the symptoms a user can see.

How the System Works

From selected symptoms to a detailed report.

1

Select Symptoms

The user ticks the symptoms seen on leaves, stem, fruit or the whole plant.

2

Analysis

The symptoms are compared with the probabilities of each disease in the dataset.

3

Result

The top matches are shown with a match score.

4

Report and History

A report with care suggestions is shown, and the result is saved in history.

About the Dataset

The project uses a tomato symptom dataset made of three files: a symptom list (31 symptoms grouped by plant part), a knowledge table with the chance of each symptom appearing in each class, and a sample dataset of 3000 records (300 per class). The 10 classes are Early Blight, Late Blight, Septoria Leaf Spot, Bacterial Spot, Leaf Mold, Spider Mites, Target Spot, Yellow Leaf Curl Virus, Mosaic Virus and Healthy.

This is a sample symptom dataset made for learning. It is not real field data.
